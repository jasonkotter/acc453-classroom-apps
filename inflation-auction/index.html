How to play

The rules are the same in every round.

Highest bids winThe number of cans is fixed for the round. Winners pay their own bid.
  • Bid at least $1 and no more than your cash.
  • Each person can win one can.
  • A tie at the cutoff is broken by a shuffled priority list.
  • No bid or no response means you keep all your cash.
  • You can revise your bid until submissions close.
